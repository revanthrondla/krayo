Frequently asked

Do I need a credit card to start a free trial?

No. You can start a 14-day free trial with just a work email — no credit card required.

Does Krayo support SSO for enterprise teams?

Yes. Krayo supports SAML-based single sign-on on the Enterprise plan, along with audit logging and role-based permissions.

Can Krayo replace our requirements traceability matrix spreadsheet?

Yes. Requirements and test cases are linked directly in the product, so the traceability matrix is generated automatically instead of maintained by hand.

How is Krayo priced?

Krayo is priced per seat on the Team plan, with custom annual contracts available on the Enterprise plan.
